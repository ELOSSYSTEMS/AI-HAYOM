These are marked discoveryFeed=true and indirectLink=true in the aggregate.
They are secondary discovery paths, not primary evidence and not claims of
verification by Google News. Reuters' public RSS delivery and AP's Media API
require licensed/authenticated access, so no private or unofficial credential
was assumed.

Policy:
- Every feed must remain HTTPS and pass bounded XML parsing.
- Primary and regulator sources carry more ranking weight than reporting feeds.
- Distinct-publisher counts are importance signals, not proof that a claim is true.
- Consequential claims require primary evidence where practical.
- The daily shortlist prioritizes the previous 24 hours, then 72-hour fallback material.
- At most one 72-168-hour context story may appear in an edition.
- The complete eligible, deduplicated feed pool is written atomically to
  output/research/aggregate-latest.json for research; it is never published directly.
